Biography of Jeon Jungkook 🐰💜

Jeon Jungkook, professionally known as Jung Kook, was born on September 1, 1997, in Busan, South Korea. He is a South Korean singer and songwriter, best known as the youngest member of the globally popular K-pop group BTS. He debuted with BTS in June 2013 under Big Hit Entertainment.

Affectionately known as the “Golden Maknae,” Jungkook earned this nickname for his remarkable talents in singing, dancing, and performing. Beyond his work with BTS, he has built a successful solo career with popular songs such as Seven, 3D, and Standing Next to You. His first solo album, GOLDEN, was released in November 2023.

Jungkook continues to inspire fans around the world with his dedication, creativity, and passion for music. To millions of fans known as ARMY, he is more than an artist—he is a source of inspiration, joy, and unforgettable memories. 💜
